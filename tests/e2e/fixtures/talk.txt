Welcome back to the channel. Today we're talking about how to edit videos faster without buying expensive software.

First, a quick story. Last year I spent eleven hours editing a single ten minute video. Eleven hours! I was exhausted, and the video still felt slow. So I changed my process completely.

Here is the surprising part. Most of my editing time was not spent cutting. It was spent searching. Searching for the best moment, searching for the right clip, searching for that one sentence I wanted to keep.

So what fixed it? Transcripts. When you have a written transcript of everything you said, you can find any moment in seconds. You search the text instead of scrubbing through the timeline.

Did you know that the average viewer decides whether to keep watching in the first three seconds? That is why your opening line matters more than anything else in the video.

My second tip is simple. Record your best point first. If you start with your strongest idea, people stay. If you start with a long introduction, people leave.

And here's my favourite trick. After recording, I mark every moment where I laughed, raised my voice, or said something unexpected. Those are almost always the clips that work as Shorts.

To sum up: use transcripts, open with your strongest idea, and look for the moments with real energy. Try it on your next video and tell me how much time you saved.
